The method

Six signals, one verdict.

We don't replace the travel sites. We read them, alongside a dozen others, and publish a single confidence-scored verdict: book, defer, or pick a different week. Everything below is evidenced, linked, and contestable.

→ 01

Microclimate record

Not the 7-day forecast. The 18-year probability distribution for your exact dates, at the pocket level — valley floor vs. ridge line, leeward vs. windward.

→ 02

Review-to-reality drift

Sentiment deltas across 40+ review surfaces, weighted by recency and verified-stay flags. We flag the moment a property, district, or experience begins quietly trending down.

→ 03

Crowd curves

Mobility models combined with transit loads, event calendars, and cruise-ship manifests. The week before "shoulder season" is often worse than peak; we show you which week is actually quiet.

→ 04

Price laddering

12-month price traces for the same room, same dates, same rate class. "Deals" measured against a property's actual median, not its inflated sticker. Identifies discount theatre.

→ 05

Access friction

Visa wait times, permit queues, road-pass seasons, regional entry caps. The bookable thing is rarely the gating thing; we surface the gate before the booking.

→ 06

Safety delta

Advisory text from seven sovereign sources, local incident reporting, and health-surveillance feeds — expressed as change since your last visit or the last 90 days. Signals, not scare.
